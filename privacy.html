1. Who we are

AnantSky LLP, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, is the “data fiduciary” (the organisation that decides why and how personal data is processed) for Aasaanly. You, the person using the app, are the “data principal” whose data this is.

Contact for privacy matters: hello@aasaanly.com · +91 80049 20005. Our Grievance Officer details are in Section 22.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Aasaanly Android app, iOS app, web app at aasaanly.com and m.aasaanly.com, our APIs, and support on phone, email and WhatsApp.

It does not cover websites or apps we do not operate (for example, our payment gateway’s checkout, maps, caller-verification, WhatsApp, or the app stores). Those companies have their own privacy policies. When Aasaanly hands data to them, this policy explains why.

Aasaanly is offered for use in India. If you use it from another country, you are responsible for complying with local law; we will still apply this policy to your personal data, and additional rights may apply under the law of your country.

4. How we collect it

  • From you — when you register, complete onboarding, post a request, set a radius, upload a photo, pay, rate, or write to support.
  • From your device — with your permission: location, camera, photos, microphone, notifications, and (optionally) contacts.
  • Automatically — technical logs, crash reports, and security events generated as you use the apps.
  • From partners — our caller-verification provider (verified number and, if shared, name); payment gateway (payment status); push-notification service (delivery); voice-AI provider (call status and transcript); messaging provider (chat delivery status); maps provider (place details you select).
  • From other users — a rating or review they write about you; the fact that they accepted or closed a request with you.

5. Why we use your data

We process personal data to:

  • create and secure your account, send OTPs, and support one-tap caller verification;
  • match end users with nearby providers by service and radius, and show live status;
  • let matched people call each other after acceptance;
  • send push, in-app, WhatsApp, SMS and (where needed) AI voice updates about requests, onboarding and safety;
  • take payments for Pro, Pro Plus, boosts, extra requests and credits, and handle refunds;
  • display and moderate profiles, photos, ratings and reviews;
  • prevent fraud, fake accounts, abuse, spam and illegal use;
  • improve matching, languages, performance and accessibility;
  • answer support tickets and resolve disputes;
  • keep accounting, tax and payment records;
  • comply with court orders, law-enforcement requests and other legal duties;
  • protect the rights, safety and property of users, AnantSky LLP and the public.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles for third-party ad networks, and we do not sell it.

6. Legal basis (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we process personal data only for a lawful purpose, with your consent or for a permitted “legitimate use”.

6.1 Consent

When you sign up, post a request that shares your number with a matched provider, allow location, upload a photo, join a voice call, or opt into WhatsApp, you give consent for those specific uses. Consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous. You may withdraw it at any time from settings or by writing to us. Withdrawal is prospective: it does not erase processing that was already done, and it may mean we cannot provide some or all of the Service (for example, nearby matching without location).

6.2 Legitimate uses (without fresh consent)

The Act allows us to process data, without separate consent, for purposes that a reasonable user would expect, including:

  • providing the Service you asked for;
  • account security, fraud prevention and network integrity;
  • mergers, acquisitions or financing, on confidentiality terms;
  • responding to a legal claim, court order or government request made under Indian law;
  • employment and internal admin at AnantSky LLP, where relevant;
  • preventing and investigating unlawful activity.

6.3 Sensitive / high-risk processing

Location, Aadhaar-based KYC (if used), payment credentials (handled by our payment gateway), and voice recordings need a higher standard of care. We collect them only when the feature requires it, keep them encrypted where feasible, and restrict internal access.

7. Location data

Location is central to Aasaanly. We use it to detect where a job is, to show providers only work inside the radius they set, to sort by distance, and to draw maps and live tracking.

  • We request coarse and/or fine location while the app is in use. We do not ask for background location.
  • You can deny location and type an address instead; matching will be less accurate.
  • A request’s location is visible to providers whose radius covers it. Your precise live GPS is not broadcast to all users.
  • Providers’ saved locations (Free: one; Pro Plus: up to five) are stored to run matching.
  • Our maps and places provider may receive search text and coordinates to show autocomplete and maps, under that provider’s terms.

8. Profile photos, KYC and moderation

Profile photos are uploaded to our image host and may sit in an “under review” state until a moderator approves them. Rejected photos are not shown to other users. Image URLs are technically accessible to anyone who has the URL; we do not publish a directory of them. Do not upload a photo you do not want public.

Where we offer Aadhaar or similar KYC for providers, we store verification data encrypted, retain at most a masked identifier (such as last-4 digits) and a verified flag, and do not display the document or full number to other users. KYC is voluntary unless a feature you want cannot work without it. You may ask us to delete KYC images after verification is complete.

9. Voice, AI agents and call data

If you speak to an Aasaanly AI voice agent, or use speech-to-text to dictate a request, we and our voice-AI provider will process audio and a transcript. We store a call record that can include the numbers involved, duration, status, agent variables and transcript, so we can complete onboarding, support you later, and investigate complaints.

AI agents can make mistakes. Do not share OTPs, full card numbers, passwords or Aadhaar on a voice call. Our agents will not ask for those.

After a match, calls between you and the other party are ordinary phone calls on your telecom network. We do not record those person-to-person calls. We may log that a call was placed from the app, if the feature exists.

10. Payments

Card, UPI, net-banking and wallet details are entered on our payment gateway’s (or the app store’s) checkout, not on our form. We receive and store a payment identifier, amount, currency, status and type (subscription, boost, credit purchase). We do not store CVV, UPI PIN or full PAN from the checkout. The gateway processes payments as an independent controller of its own customer data; read the gateway’s policy on the checkout page.

We share personal data only as described below. We do not sell it.

11.1 With the other party to a job

When a provider accepts, or an end user accepts a provider, the app may share the phone number and first name needed to talk and complete the job, plus the request details and approximate location. KYC documents, full birth date, and payment instruments are not shared.

11.4 Legal and safety

We may disclose data to lawyers, auditors, courts, tribunals, police or government authorities in India if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent harm, fraud or a serious offence.

11.5 Business transfer

If AnantSky LLP is acquired, merges, or sells assets, user data may be part of the transfer. We will require the successor to honour this policy, and will notify you where the law requires.

12. Cross-border transfers

Most processing (database, API, Indian payments, Indian SMS) happens in India or on infrastructure we control for Indian users. Some processors — maps, messaging, image hosting, caller verification and parts of cloud hosting — may store or process data in Singapore, the United States, the European Union or elsewhere.

Under the DPDP Act we may transfer personal data to another country unless the Central Government has restricted that country. We do not transfer data to a restricted country. We use contractual controls and access limits for overseas processors.

13. Cookies, identifiers and similar technologies

The web app and website may use cookies and similar browser storage to keep you logged in, remember language, and understand traffic. Native apps use device identifiers and push tokens instead of cookies.

You can clear cookies in your browser. Blocking all cookies may break login on the web. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies on aasaanly.com.

14. How we protect data

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • HTTPS in transit;
  • encryption of sensitive identifiers such as mobile numbers and KYC data at rest;
  • authentication on APIs; short-lived sessions; OTP expiry;
  • least-privilege access for staff and contractors, and audit logs;
  • photo moderation before public display;
  • payment secrets kept server-side, never in the app bundle;
  • dependency updates, monitoring and a process for security reports.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to hello@aasaanly.com (see also our Security page). Do not test against other users.

As required by the IT Act and SPDI Rules, we maintain reasonable security practices for sensitive personal data. Access inside AnantSky LLP is limited to people who need it to do their job.

15. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose, or as the law requires:

  • Account data: for the life of the account, and a short period after deletion for safety and to honour a deletion request cleanly.
  • OTP codes: until they expire (minutes), then deleted; we may keep a log that an OTP was sent.
  • Work requests and ratings: while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for dispute and trust-and-safety purposes, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax, accounting and payment-industry rules require (typically several years after the transaction).
  • Voice recordings / transcripts: for a limited period (generally not more than 12 months, unless needed for an ongoing dispute or legal hold).
  • Push tokens: until you log out, delete the app, or ask us to remove them.
  • Security logs: typically 12 months.

When we no longer need data, we delete it or irreversibly anonymise it so it no longer identifies you.

16. Your rights

As a data principal under the DPDP Act, 2023 — and, where applicable, under the SPDI Rules — you may:

  1. Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you, the processing done, and the identities of other fiduciaries with whom it has been shared, as the Act requires us to tell you.
  2. Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  3. Erasure — ask us to delete data that is no longer needed for the purpose, or that you withdrew consent for (subject to legal retention).
  4. Grievance redressal — complain to our Grievance Officer (Section 22), and, if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India once operational.
  5. Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to do so (for example, in case of death or incapacity).
  6. Withdraw consent for future optional processing.
  7. Portability-lite: we can provide the profile and request data you gave us in a readable format on request, to the extent the Act and our systems allow.

We will verify that the request is from you (usually by OTP on the registered number). We will not discriminate against you for exercising rights.

Some rights are limited: we cannot delete data we must keep by law, data needed to resolve an ongoing dispute, or data that would identify a person who reported abuse if deletion would put others at risk.

16.1 How to make a request

Email hello@aasaanly.com with the subject “Privacy request — access / correction / deletion / nomination”, from an address we can match, or send the request from the registered mobile number. We will respond within the DPDP timelines once the Board’s rules are fully in force, and in any case within the one-month grievance period under the e-commerce rules.

16.2 If you are outside India

EU/UK/California-style rights (such as a formal GDPR Article 15 request or a CCPA “know/sell” request) are not the primary framework for Aasaanly. We do not sell personal data, so a “do not sell” flag is not needed. If such a law applies to you, write to us and we will consider the request in good faith, on top of your DPDP rights.

17. Children

Aasaanly is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it unless a parent or legal guardian consents and the use remains lawful. Under the DPDP Act, processing a child’s data generally requires verifiable parental consent and must not be detrimental to the child’s well-being. We do not run tracking or targeted advertising directed at children.

18. Sensitive personal data and special categories

Under the SPDI Rules, “sensitive personal data or information” includes passwords, financial information, health information, biometric information and official identifiers. On Aasaanly this can include:

  • payment status records and, at the gateway, card/UPI details;
  • Aadhaar-based KYC, if you choose to verify;
  • precise location;
  • voice audio.

We collect these only with consent or another lawful basis, with a stated purpose, and with the security measures in Section 14. You may withdraw consent for optional sensitive processing (KYC, voice, contacts) without it affecting the rest of the Service where technically possible.

Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN or card images on WhatsApp or email unless we ask for them for a specific verification.

19. Notifications, WhatsApp and marketing

  • Push notifications can be turned off in device settings. Critical request updates may still appear in-app.
  • WhatsApp — reply STOP, or ask us to stop. Live-job messages may continue while a request is open.
  • SMS is used mainly for OTP. Marketing SMS, if any, will include a way to opt out.
  • Email is used for account and support. Promotional email, if we start it, will have an unsubscribe link.

20. If a breach happens

If personal data is compromised in a manner required to be reported, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India (and any other authority the law names) without undue delay, with the information the law requires: what happened, what data, likely consequences, and what we and you can do.

21. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, the law, or our vendors change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. For material changes we will also try to notify you in the app or by an available channel. The latest version on this page is the one that applies.
