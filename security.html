Security

Aasaanly is used inside homes and on people’s phones. Security has to work on everyday Indian networks and entry-level devices. This page explains what we do — and what you can do — to keep accounts, payments and personal data safe.

How we protect the platform

  • HTTPS for all app and web traffic.
  • Authentication on APIs; short-lived sessions after OTP login.
  • Least-privilege access for staff, with audit logs.
  • Server-side secrets for Razorpay, Sarvam, WhatsApp and the database — never inside the app bundle.
  • Regular dependency updates, monitoring, and graceful failure on poor networks.
  • Photo and review moderation before or after they go live.

Read the Privacy Policy for the legal side of data processing, and the Terms for platform rules.

Accounts and OTP login

There are no passwords to leak. Login is a 4-digit OTP sent to your mobile number. Truecaller one-tap is available where supported. OTPs expire quickly. If someone uses your number without permission, contact us and we will help lock the account.

Payments

Card, UPI and net-banking details are entered on Razorpay’s (or the app store’s) checkout, not stored by Aasaanly. We keep only order IDs, amounts and status, so we can activate Pro, boosts and credits and process refunds. Payment secrets stay on the server.

Data handling

Mobile numbers and KYC data (where used) are stored in encrypted form. Precise live GPS is not broadcast to all users. We do not sell personal data. Internal access is limited to people who need it to run the Service.

Profile photos and reviews

Profile photos may remain hidden until a moderator approves them. Ratings and reviews can be removed if they are fake, abusive or illegal. Report a user or a request from the app, or email us with screenshots.

Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide Aasaanly, resolve disputes, or meet Indian tax and accounting law. Details and timelines are in the Privacy Policy.

Responsible disclosure

If you find a vulnerability, please tell us before public disclosure. Email hello@aasaanly.com with:

  • a short description and impact;
  • steps to reproduce, or a proof-of-concept;
  • affected app or URL;
  • your contact (we will credit you if you want).

Please do not access, change or delete other people’s data, and do not run denial- of-service tests. We will acknowledge your report and work to resolve valid issues promptly. We do not currently run a paid bug bounty.

What you can do

  • Keep your phone and SIM secure; a lost SIM can receive your OTP.
  • Do not share OTPs — Aasaanly staff and our AI agents will never ask for them.
  • Check a provider’s ratings before you accept, and meet sensibly.
  • Turn off permissions you do not want (location, contacts, microphone).
  • Report harassment, fraud or a safety issue immediately.
